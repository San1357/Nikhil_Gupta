I'm a Senior Data Scientist at QuEST Global in Pune. For about 7 years I've worked on getting deep learning models out of notebooks and onto real hardware: traffic cameras on poles, parking lots, cars, and ECG machines.

My favourite part of the job is the last mile. A model that scores well on a validation set is half the work; the other half is freezing, optimising and converting it with TensorRT or OpenVINO until it runs in real time on an Nvidia Jetson or an Intel box. Along the way I've also built forecasting models, a literature search engine and topic models.

I studied Computer Science at IIT Mandi. Outside work I've played basketball at the Inter-IIT meet, gone on treks with the campus hiking club, and picked up a green belt in kickboxing.

Projects

Client work, so names are anonymised where needed.

Wrong Way Detection

Leading ADAS provider

Flags cars driving against traffic from a roadside camera and pushes a snapshot to Azure. Mini YOLOv3 for detection, a Kalman tracker per vehicle, and optical flow to compare each car's motion with the lane's expected direction.

Darknet · TensorRT · OpenCV · Jetson · Docker

Lane-wise Traffic Analysis

Leading ADAS provider

Counts vehicles per lane and estimates congestion in real time. VGG-SegNet segments the lanes, OpenCV fits lane lines, and a modified YOLOv3 counts vehicles, with both networks merged into one TensorRT graph.

Keras · TensorFlow · TensorRT · OpenCV

Road Marking Segmentation

Leading automotive OEM

Pixel-wise segmentation of arrows, lines and crossings for autonomous driving and city mapping. Swapped SegNet and U-Net encoders for pre-trained VGG16, which converged faster and scored better on IoU.

Keras · VGG-SegNet · VGG-U-Net · TensorRT · Jetson

ECG Arrhythmia Detection

Leading European healthcare OEM

A 1D residual CNN classifies heartbeats as normal or arrhythmic. A variational autoencoder trained only on healthy ECGs then scores severity: the further a signal is from "normal", the more severe.

TensorFlow · 1D CNN · VAE · OpenVINO

Cafe Sales Forecasting

Cafe chain

Next-day sales forecasts per menu item from 2 years of sales, stock and wastage data, so each outlet orders the right amount from the kitchen. DeepAR on SageMaker, with holidays and temperature as extra signals.

SageMaker · DeepAR · Pandas · S3

Smart Parking Detection

Leading US telecom operator

Finds free spots in a parking lot from a single camera by detecting parked cars and mapping their boxes onto the slot layout. Deployed on Jetson after converting the TensorFlow model to TensorRT.

TensorFlow · TensorRT · OpenCV

Search & Author Topic Modelling

Elsevier, Netherlands

A Solr search engine over journals and books with auto-suggest, highlighting and filters, plus an LDA-based author-topic model: type a researcher's name to see their topics, or a topic to see who works on it.

Solr · Lucene · Gensim · spaCy · LDA

Also: traffic sign classification, aeroplane door scratch detection.
